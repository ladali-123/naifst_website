OUR PURPOSE

Vision, mission and values that guide our work

NAISFT INDIA focuses on practical learning, career guidance, digital support, transparent certification and responsible growth of its training network.

01

Our Vision

To become one of India’s most trusted training academies in industrial safety, fire technology and skill development by creating a professional education network that supports students, professionals and franchise partners.

02

Our Mission

Provide industry-oriented training, develop practical workplace knowledge, support students with career guidance, maintain digital verification and student portals, and expand skill-based education through a growing franchise network.

03

Our Core Values

Integrity, quality, practicality, student success and innovation guide how we train, support learners, manage records and improve the overall student experience.
