CERTIFICATE VERIFICATION FAQ

Common verification questions

Clear guidance for students, employers, recruiters and institutions checking a NAISFT credential.

What happens when I scan the QR code?+

The QR code opens a NAISFT verification route associated with the certificate token. The website checks that token against the certificate record and shows the result.

Can I verify without scanning the QR code?+

Yes. Enter the unique certificate token manually in the instant verification field.

What should I do if a certificate cannot be found?+

Check the token carefully for typing errors. If the issue continues, contact NAISFT support using the details below.

Can employers or recruiters use this page?+

Yes. This verification page can be used by authorised third parties checking a certificate presented by a learner, subject to NAISFT's privacy and verification policies.
